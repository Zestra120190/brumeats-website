About the show

Hey, I’m Jack. I deliver food around Birmingham on my bike — rain, shine, or absolute chaos.

Expect honest vlogs about life on the road: long waits, weird orders, near misses, and the odd brilliant moment that makes it all worth it.

No flashy edits or fake motivation. Just what it’s actually like earning a living on two wheels. If you’ve ever wondered what happens between “order placed” and “order delivered” — stick around.
